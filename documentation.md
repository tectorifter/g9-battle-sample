# g9-battle-sample — manifest description

This file records the full `description` for the **g9-battle-sample** mod
(`manifest.json`). The manifest's own `description` field is capped at
**80 characters** (a studio rule, so the Mod Manager row and the generator
listing stay readable), so the complete text lives here and the manifest
carries only a short summary.

## Current description (full)

TEST/EXAMPLE mod, not a real feature -- fork of Sample-Battle-Scene retargeted at g9-Battle-Scene instead of g2-Battle-Scene, so the g9-battle-engine connection (turn resolution, EXP/catch, the battle-exit music sequence) has a real, live encounter to exercise. Works on BOTH generations (Red/Blue/Yellow and Gold/Silver/Crystal). With the 2v2/3v3/4v1 row on (the default) every wild encounter renders through g9-Battle-Scene -- the default band to its 'singles' preset (so the vanilla battle screen is replaced outright), with 10%/2%/1% bands to 'doubles'/'triples'/'hordes' and a 0.5% 'bossFight' band (x1-x5 max HP, random boss protections). With that row OFF every fight still renders through the scene, but always on its 'singles' layout; switching the custom scene off entirely (native battles) is g9-Battle-Scene's own BACKGROUND row set to OFF. A trainer battle -- a first fight OR a rematch -- picks its scene layout from the enemy battle that already exists: a 2+-mon team is 'doubles', the Elite Four roll doubles-or-triples, the Champion and Red are 'bossFight', and a single-Pokemon team takes the scene's own 'singles' screen (never the game's native one). Options add a trainer difficulty preset (easy/normal/hard/hell) -- which sets both each mon's IVs/EVs/nature and a team-size floor (easy: vanilla, normal: 2, hard: 4, hell: 6; Pokemon are only ever added when the vanilla team is under the floor, at the team's own level) -- and two toggles -- RAND WILDS and RAND TRAINER MONS -- which re-roll species to type-matching, BST-equivalent ones from the registered national dex and then evolve the pick to the latest form its level allows (a level-up evolution needs its own level, a trade one needs level 40, a stone evolution appears in the wild only from level 35, and a held-item or happiness evolution appears from level 35 too (or level 25 when the mon evolving is a baby); a legendary is only ever chosen at level 45 or higher, and a blacklisted species (Mega and Gigantamax forms are delisted out of the box) is never picked at all). A wild encounter rolled while surfing OR fishing only ever re-rolls into a WATER- or FLYING-type species -- water areas are water-and-flying exclusive -- while a land or cave encounter bans WATER types (a FLYING type is welcome anywhere). Do NOT enable alongside Sample-Battle-Scene at the same time -- both mods independently wrap World.tryWildEncounter, and having two mods intercept the same encounter conflicts. Six more options: ITEM RANDOMIZER (every ordinary item ball and hidden item holds a random non-key item from the whole item list instead of its real one -- a spot that really held a key item is left exactly as it is, TMs and HMs are in the pool); ITEM RESPAWN (one random already-taken non-key item on the current map comes back, once per RESPAWN TIMER -- 30s or 1-5 minutes -- an item ball placed back where it was, a hidden item diggable again); REMATCHES (press A on an already-beaten trainer and they ask whether you want to battle again -- YES starts a rematch, NO carries on with their usual after-battle line; rematches only ever start on your own press, rewards never pay twice, and story-scene trainers are left alone); +LEVEL PER REMATCH (off/+1/+2/+4/+8/+16, cumulative on each Pokemon in that trainer's team, one step per win); and TRAINER ITEM DROP (beating any trainer can hand you one random non-key item, at the TRAINER ITEM CHANCE -- 5/10/20/40/80/100%, 100% by default). And a BATTLE SCENE toggle: ON (the default) keeps every wild encounter and trainer battle on g9-Battle-Scene's custom layouts; OFF puts EVERY fight -- wild, the 0.5% rolling boss, hordes, triples, doubles, the custom singles screen, every trainer battle and every rematch -- back on the game's own native battle screen, for Gen 1 and Gen 2 respectively, and g9-Battle-Scene is never consulted (not even by another mod's registered trainer), which is why it is an optional dependency, not a hard one. A BLACKLIST row on the OPTIONS menu opens a bigger custom window -- a native Game Boy border filling a 320x180 surface (the whole 960x540 playfield at an exact 3x, 16:9) -- that indexes every Pokemon and alternate form by name, filters by generation (START) and starting letter (SELECT), walks the name list and the generation grid as one horizontal run on LEFT/RIGHT (the list, then G1 > G2 > G3, then back to the list), and delists a species, a form, or a whole generation's roster from randomization (the list is saved; Mega and Gigantamax forms are delisted out of the box, everything else starts in), and a [MEGA] [GIGA] row under the generation grid flips either whole form group at once with RESET ALL pushed below it; a delisted species is never picked. The window declares itself the engine's wide-UI owner, so Gen 1's Game:draw sizes its 320x180 surface for it and on Gen 2 it supplies that generation's own wide-panel contract, and it never breaks out of the player's screen. The TRAIN party editor (IV/EV/Nature/gender and the movepool editor) now lives in g9-battle-engine, behind its TRAIN SCREEN row. A stone evolution only appears in the wild from level 35, a water area is WATER-and-FLYING exclusive, a water type is banned inland, and a flying type is allowed everywhere. All of them work on both generations. Since 1.1.0 Mega and Gigantamax forms are delisted out of the box (so the randomizer never turns a wild roll or a trainer's mon into one), the BLACKLIST window's generation grid gains a [MEGA] [GIGA] row that flips either whole group at once with RESET ALL pushed below it, and a held-item or happiness evolution now appears in the wild from level 35 -- or level 25 when the mon evolving is a BABY (Happiny's Chansey) -- where it previously needed level 40. Delisting a Mega or Gigantamax form is a RANDOMIZER filter only: it never touches a battle-triggered form change, so a boss fight's own mega evolution still fires as the player enters the scene. Since 1.2.0 gym leaders and the Elite Four are re-fightable behind a dedicated GYM REMATCHES toggle (default on): with it on -- and REMATCHES on -- a beaten leader asks whether you want to battle again and, on YES, starts the rematch WITHOUT re-running its hand-ported badge/TM script (a badge or TM is still never paid twice), while with it off the league keeps its story talk; every gym leader's, Elite Four member's and gym trainer's party -- a randomized swap AND a difficulty-added member, a first fight and a rematch alike -- is held to that gym's own type theme (GYM THEMES), so Falkner only ever fields FLYING types, Brock only ROCK, Lorelei only ICE and so on (a species carrying at least one of the gym's types is legal, and a mixed-type class like Blue is unrestricted); a wild DOUBLES or TRIPLES fight now rolls each extra enemy independently (it may repeat a species, but each slot is a fresh roll) while a horde still clones one species and level; and two wild settings -- WILD LEVELS (OFF default, or 0/+2/+4/+6/+8/+16 and -2/-4/-6/-8/-16) and WILD LV ANCHOR (LOWEST/HIGHEST/AVERAGE/LEADER), read only while RAND WILDS is on -- rebase every wild Pokemon's level onto a party anchor while keeping the area's own spawn-level spread, so a +16 over a party whose highest is 30 turns a route's 2-4 window into 46-48 and a 0 makes that same 2-4 route read 30-32. Since 1.2.1 WILD LEVELS has an explicit OFF (now the default) that truly leaves wild Pokemon at their vanilla levels, while 0 really rebases them onto the anchor instead of silently doing nothing. Since 1.2.2 the GYM REMATCHES toggle actually reaches the gym leaders and Elite Four on Gold/Silver/Crystal: a G/S/C leader is an OBJECTTYPE_SCRIPT object (its fight lives in its own map script, not in a trainer struct), so the rematch gate never saw one and only the ordinary trainers around it asked -- now a beaten leader there asks 'Do you want to battle again?' exactly like a beaten trainer (YES starts a rematch of its team, honoring the +LEVEL ramp and its gym's type theme, without re-running the badge/TM rows; NO runs the leader's own script, so its advice line shows as usual), and a G/S/C trainer object's numeric trainer class is now resolved to its class name so GYM THEMES/GYM REMATCHES key correctly. Since 1.2.3 the Ruins of Alph underground -- the four item chambers and the Inner Chamber, the Unown spawn area -- is never randomised, and there is deliberately no setting to turn that ban off: a wild (or fished) roll made there is handed on exactly as the game rolled it, species and level untouched, so Unown keep spawning there while the Ruins exterior (Natu/Smeargle/Wooper) stays randomised as an ordinary area. Since 1.2.4 the wild randomizer never hands out an HM, a Mega Stone, a Z-Crystal, or one of the four battle key items (KEY_STONE, DYNAMAX_BAND, Z_RING, TERA_ORB): those families are excluded from the item pool outright, so no random item ball, hidden item or trainer drop can produce one (and an ordinary evolution stone is unaffected). Also since 1.2.4 a randomised wild Pokemon evolves as far as the level its own VANILLA spawn table rolled -- WILD LEVELS still rebases the level the fight happens at, but it no longer pushes the species further up its evolution line (or widens its band) than that spawn level allows. Since 1.3.0 LEAGUE ACES -- ON by default, and offered only while RAND TRAINER MONS is on -- pins each gym leader's, Elite Four member's, Champion's and Red's signature Pokemon to the FINAL slots of their team so the randomizer cannot replace them: the early slots keep their randomized/padded team, an ace takes the level of the slot it fills, and while aces keep their ORIGINAL MOVESETS their nature/IVs/EVs come from DIFFICULTY like any other trainer mon (except Lance's Dragonite, which keeps its own special+speed spread); the held item is the one named (Eviolite, a Mega Stone, Light Ball) and Mega, Gigantamax, Dynamax and Tera are stored as properties for battle_forms to deploy, exactly like a wild SPECIAL BOSS, so a delisted Mega form still cannot stop a boss's own transformation. Gen 1's Champion is one of three sets -- Yellow rolls one fresh each battle, while Red/Blue picks by the player's starter (Squirtle -> the Venusaur set, Charmander -> the Blastoise set, Bulbasaur -> the Charizard set). Since 1.4.0 LEAGUE ACES is a real Mod Manager setting as well: `options.lua`'s `league_aces` row renders in the manager's options list ONLY while RAND TRAINER MONS is on (the schema's own `visible_if` field, so the row appears and disappears live as that row is flipped -- never while the randomizer is off, because the aces protect slots the randomizer would otherwise take), the in-game OPTIONS menu carries the same ON/OFF row, and an explicit in-session flip there wins over the manager's configured value.

## Manifest summary (<= 80 chars)

Sample mod: routes battles through the g9 engine and scene, with options.

  **tectorifter** (<https://github.com/tectorifter/>). No code change.
- **1.4.1** localizes the rematch question. `Do you want to battle again?` is
  this mod's own string, so the translation mod never sees it; a new local
  `rematchPrompt()` resolves g9-gui's translation layer
  (`mod.find("g9-gui").exports.translation.line`) and is passed to the two
  `world:showText` calls and the Gen 2 `TextBox.new` in place of the literal.
  Fail-open: without g9-gui, or with the layer's TRANSLATION row off, the
  English question stands.
- Full text moved here and the manifest `description` shortened to the
  summary above. Doc/description-only change, so no version bump.
- **1.4.0** makes **LEAGUE ACES** a real Mod Manager setting: `options.lua`
  (and the identical table `main.lua` passes to `mod.options:define`) now
  carries a `league_aces` toggle, default **ON**, hidden by the schema's own
  `visible_if` unless **RAND TRAINER MONS** is on -- so the row appears and
  disappears live as that row is flipped. The manager's value is what the
  applier reads (the in-game OPTIONS-menu row is the in-session flip, and an
  explicit flip there wins).
- **1.3.0** adds the **LEAGUE ACES** row (only while RAND TRAINER MONS is on,
  ON by default): each gym leader / Elite Four / Champion / Red keeps their
  listed signature Pokemon at the END of their team, exempt from the
  randomizer, with original movesets and DIFFICULTY-driven nature/IVs/EVs and
  the named held item; Gen 1's Champion is three sets (Yellow rolls one per
  battle, Red/Blue picks by the player's starter). The full team table is in
  `LEAGUE_ACES.md`.
- **1.2.5** renamed the sample's `battle_scene` row to **2v2/3v3/4v1** (it now
  picks the layout: OFF = the scene's singles screen, not native) and moved the
  scene's master on/off to `g9-Battle-Scene`'s **BACKGROUND** row (OFF = native
  battles).
